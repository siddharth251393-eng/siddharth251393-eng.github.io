India’s 10 GWh battery tender draws 22 bids; earlier scheme had installed 1 GWh by March

Proposed byline: IndustryWire Editorial Desk · Drafted 17 November 2026 · Not yet published

Twenty-two companies have bid for India’s latest 10 GWh advanced-chemistry-cell manufacturing tender for grid-scale storage, the Ministry of Heavy Industries said. A ministry status table published in March showed 1 GWh installed against 40 GWh previously awarded under the wider battery incentive scheme.

The new tender is earmarked for grid-scale stationary storage and forms part of the government’s 50 GWh production-linked incentive programme. Technical bids were opened on 11 November after the tender closed the previous day. The ministry said selection will use a two-stage quality- and cost-based process; it did not name selected firms or provide an award timetable in its 12 November announcement.

Installed capacity remains the key measure to watch

A ministry reply published on 27 March listed 40 GWh awarded to four beneficiary companies and 1 GWh installed. Its table attributed the installed 1 GWh to Ola Cell Technologies; the ministry said the plant had begun pilot production and was working towards stabilising full-scale commercial production. It also said domestic demand for advanced chemistry cells continued to be met largely through imports.

That March status is not a current November progress figure: the new 22-bid announcement does not update installation totals for the earlier awards. The bid count therefore shows participation in the new procurement round, not how much production capacity is operating or how quickly earlier projects have progressed. The ministry’s next project-status update will be needed to judge whether the gap between awards and installed capacity has narrowed.

Who submitted bids?

The ministry’s list includes ACME Solar Holdings, Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies, Avaada Electro, Exide Energy Solutions, JSW Neo Energy, Reliance Industries, Vikram Solar and Waaree Energy Storage Solutions, among 22 bidders. A submitted bid does not mean a company has won capacity or committed a final investment.
