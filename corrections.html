How to report an issue

Email the editorial desk at . Include the article URL, the passage in question, and any supporting documents or links. We review submissions and may contact you for clarification.

Sending a correction request does not guarantee a particular outcome. We assess the evidence and, where a material error is confirmed, update the article with a correction note and date.
