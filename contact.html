GET IN TOUCH

Contact IndustryWire

Use the form to prepare an email in your own mail app. You can also express interest in contributing articles, future company announcements or media partnerships. These are enquiries only; paid publishing orders, subscriptions and partner distribution are not open.

Email-based fallback

Submitting opens a draft addressed to the IndustryWire contact inbox. Review the draft and send it from your email application. A mail app must be configured on your device.

For an announcement enquiry, include a short summary and links to public supporting sources. For a media partnership enquiry, describe your organization, audience, markets and proposed collaboration. For contributor interest, share relevant experience and public writing. Please do not send confidential information or attachments in your first email.

Your details stay in this browser until the email draft opens. Nothing is transmitted until you send the email.
