STANDARDS & PRACTICES

Editorial policy

Publication standard · Page updated 16 November 2026

These standards describe how The Sector Journal intends to report and handle material as the publication develops.

Accuracy and verification

For independent newsroom reporting, we seek to verify factual claims using primary documents, official statements, regulatory filings, company disclosures and named sources. We distinguish confirmed facts from estimates, allegations, forecasts and opinion. Company claims are attributed and are not presented as independently verified unless they have been checked.

Sources and originality

We aim to link to material sources where available, provide enough context for readers to assess a claim, and produce original reporting and analysis. We do not copy another publisher’s reporting. Submitted material is reviewed for factual support, originality, public interest and rights to text, images and data.

Independence and conflicts

Independent newsroom decisions remain separate from the paid publishing service, advertisers, sponsors and research buyers. Payment for client publishing does not buy independent coverage, favourable coverage, search ranking or inclusion in Google News. Potential conflicts are disclosed and managed by the editor.

Sponsored material and press releases

Paid client publishing is a separate service in development. If accepted, promotional or client-supplied material will be labelled clearly and prominently, for example “Sponsored” or “Press Release”. It will not be presented as independent reporting. Requests may be declined and payment will not control newsroom coverage or conclusions. No external syndication, publication deadline, audience size or search outcome is promised.

AI assistance

AI tools may assist research or editing. A human editor remains responsible for checking facts, quotations, sources, images, rights and the final publication. Unreviewed AI-generated news is not published.

Corrections and updates

Material errors should be corrected promptly and transparently. Significant corrections are explained on the affected article and recorded on the corrections page. Updates that do not alter a material fact may be noted in the article.

Authorship

Published articles should identify the responsible author or newsroom. Author biographies must describe experience accurately and must not imply credentials that the author does not hold.

Reader feedback

Readers may send corrections, evidence, questions or concerns to the editorial desk.
