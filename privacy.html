Privacy notice

Website launch-stage notice · 16 November 2026

This notice describes the current static website and its contact options. Features may change; we will update this page if we add analytics, newsletter tools or a hosted form.

Information collected through this website

This site has no account system, analytics service, advertising pixels, newsletter signup backend or server-side contact form. The site does not intentionally set tracking cookies. GitHub states that when its Pages sites are visited, visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact enquiries

The contact form works in your browser to prepare a message in your email application. The website does not transmit or store the form. If you choose to send the email, your email provider will transmit it to IndustryWire’s contact inbox. The information you include may identify you or your organisation. Do not send confidential or sensitive information unless necessary.

Newsletter and third parties

No newsletter subscription service is currently active, and no mailing list is collected through this site. External links open third-party websites that operate under their own privacy terms.

Retention and requests

Messages that you choose to send are handled in the receiving email service. A separate retention schedule has not been established for this launch-stage publication. To ask about a message you sent, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy question, email the IndustryWire contact address.

Changes

This notice will be revised if the site adds a new data-collection feature or service provider. Please check this page for updates.
