Privacy notice

Website notice · 17 November 2026

This notice explains the information handled by the Sector Journal website as it is currently built. The Sector Journal is a publication in development; its legal publishing entity has not been identified on this site.

Contact enquiries

The contact form prepares an email draft in your own email application. The site does not submit or store the form. If you choose to send the draft, your email provider transmits it to the Sector Journal contact inbox. The site may record a limited analytics event that an email draft was opened, if you first accepted optional analytics. That event does not confirm that the email was sent and does not include your form field contents.

Do not include confidential or sensitive information in an initial enquiry. No file uploads, account system, paid publishing orders or subscriptions are currently available.

Optional analytics

When Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are configured on a site version, they load only after you choose “Accept optional analytics.” You can reject them or change your choice using “Privacy settings” in the footer. If you reject, the optional tags are not loaded. The choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it.

After consent, these providers may process information such as pages viewed, browser and device details, referral information, approximate location, and clicks or scrolling. This helps us understand which pages are useful and where the site can improve. We do not intentionally send contact form text, email addresses entered in forms, or the contents of email drafts as analytics events. Microsoft Clarity masks form input text by default; see its privacy and masking documentation.

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are separate services with their own privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement. A preview may have analytics disabled until its project IDs are configured. The consent controls remain inactive when no analytics service is configured.

Hosting and service operation

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser or device details, requested page and time, for delivery, security and service operation under its own terms and privacy notice. The provider depends on the site address you visit: GitHub Pages data collection or Netlify privacy statement.

Retention and privacy enquiries

Messages you choose to send are handled in the receiving email service. The Sector Journal has not established a separate retention schedule for this launch-stage site. To ask about a message you sent or raise a privacy question, email the Sector Journal contact address.

Changes

We will update this notice before enabling a new data-collection feature or service provider. Check this page for changes.
