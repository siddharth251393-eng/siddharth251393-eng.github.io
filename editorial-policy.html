Editorial policy

Accuracy and verification

We seek to verify factual claims using primary sources, official statements, regulatory filings, company disclosures and named expert sources. We distinguish confirmed facts from estimates, allegations and opinion.

Originality

We do not copy articles from other publishers. Submitted material is reviewed for originality, factual support, rights to text and images, and public interest. We may reject or edit submissions.

Independence and conflicts

Editorial decisions are made independently of advertisers and paying contributors. Commercial relationships do not guarantee publication, favourable coverage, search ranking or Google News visibility.

Sponsored material and press releases

Paid or promotional content is clearly labelled (for example, “Sponsored” or “Press Release”). It is not presented as independent reporting. Sponsorship disclosures are placed prominently. Paid content is subject to review and may be declined.

AI assistance

AI tools may assist research or editing, but a human editor is responsible for checking facts, sources, quotations, images and the final article. We do not publish unreviewed AI-generated news.

Corrections

Material errors are corrected promptly. Significant corrections are explained in the article and logged on our corrections page.

Authorship

Articles identify the responsible author or newsroom. Author biographies describe relevant experience accurately and do not imply credentials that the author does not hold.

Reader feedback

Readers may contact the editorial desk with evidence, corrections or concerns using the contact details on this website.
