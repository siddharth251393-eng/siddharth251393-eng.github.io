India’s wholesale inflation rises to 12.29% as Fuel & Power rate accelerates

By The Sector Journal Editorial Desk · Published 17 November 2026 at 3:26 pm IST

India’s wholesale price inflation accelerated to 12.29% year on year in October from 11.39% in September, according to provisional data released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry on 16 November. The Fuel and Power rate rose sharply, while annual inflation in Manufactured Products edged lower.

The figures describe different movements inside the same index. They do not show how prices changed at every company or what happened to any individual manufacturer’s costs or margins.

Fuel and Power recorded the steepest rate increase

Among the WPI’s three major groups, Fuel and Power inflation increased by 6.99 percentage points, to 32.84% year on year from 25.85%. Primary Articles inflation rose by 0.57 points to 9.24%. Manufactured Products inflation moved in the other direction, easing by 0.22 points to 9.54%.

The ministry’s release lists multiple items as drivers of October WPI inflation, including mineral oils, crude petroleum and natural gas, electricity, food articles and several manufactured categories. The published group rates show where annual inflation accelerated or eased; they should not be read as a ranking of individual companies’ cost increases.

Manufacturing’s two indicators moved in different directions

The annual inflation rate for Manufactured Products eased from 9.76% to 9.54%. In the same release, a separate trial Input Producer Price Index for manufacturing rose from 106.1 in September to 108.2 in October, a 2.1-point increase in the index.

The Input PPI is a trial aggregate index, not a direct record of what every factory paid for materials or energy. The divergence therefore does not prove that manufacturers’ margins widened or narrowed. Company sourcing, contracts and product mixes differ, and the release does not provide firm-level evidence.

What the 12.29% rate compares

The October inflation rate compares the All Commodities WPI index with its level in October a year earlier. It is not the percentage change from September to October. The index itself rose from 112.5 in September to 113.3 in October; that index-level movement is a separate comparison from the 12.29% year-on-year rate.

The WPI Food Index, which combines food articles and manufactured food products, recorded 8.58% year-on-year inflation in October, compared with 8.66% in September. It eased even as the overall WPI rate rose.

October estimate remains provisional

The ministry says the provisional October WPI was compiled at a weighted response rate of 81.1%. It has scheduled the November WPI, Output PPI and trial Input PPI release for 14 December 2026. October figures may be revised as more information becomes available.

Sources

  • Ministry of Commerce and Industry, October 2026 WPI, Output PPI and trial Input PPI release, 16 November 2026 — headline and group inflation rates, index levels, trial Input PPI, provisional status, response rate and release schedule.
  • Office of the Economic Adviser: WPI item-wise data, base 2022–23.
Data note

October 2026 estimates are provisional and may be revised. Percentage-point changes compare year-on-year inflation rates; index-point changes refer to the index itself. This analysis of an official statistical release does not establish company-specific costs or margins.
