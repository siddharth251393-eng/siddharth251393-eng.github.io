ABOUT THE PUBLICATION

An independent newsroom for business and industry.

The Sector Journal is building an independent business and industry newsroom. Clearly labelled B2B publishing may be explored as a long-term business model after original coverage, reader trust and demand are established.

We are building a publishing desk for communications teams, PR agencies and research publishers who need a clear place to introduce relevant industry material. Planned newsroom coverage spans manufacturing, energy, healthcare, technology and business, with an Indian market perspective and an international outlook.

Mission

To make useful industry information easier to find while keeping paid client publishing visibly separate from independent reporting.

Editorial approach

The independent newsroom will verify claims against primary documents and credible sources, seek comment where appropriate, identify responsible authors and correct material errors transparently. Paid client material will be labelled clearly and will not determine editorial conclusions.

Publisher information

The Sector Journal is being developed from India for readers in India and international markets. “The Sector Journal” is the publication name; Kavita is the named publisher, not the firm’s owner. The owner and legal publisher/operator structure and any registered business details have not yet been established on this website. See the publisher profile for her role. The publishing service is in development; no distribution partners or audience figures are claimed. We do not claim a staff size, readership, circulation history, years of operation or industry membership.

Bylines

Desk-produced explainers may use the transparent The Sector Journal Editorial Desk organizational byline. Individual contributors will be named only when their identity and contribution are confirmed.

Who we serve

The publication is designed to serve industry executives, investors and analysts, and corporate communications teams with sourced reporting and useful market context. If a clearly labelled paid publishing service is developed later, communications teams may also submit eligible material; that service is not currently open.

Interested in contributing?

If you write about business or industry—or would like to help shape the editorial team as it develops—tell us about your subject areas, relevant experience and public writing samples. Enquiries are expressions of interest only; roles and assignments are not guaranteed or currently advertised.
