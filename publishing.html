LONG-TERM BUSINESS PLAN

Publishing options may come later.

IndustryWire is currently focused on building an independent industry newsroom and reader trust. Paid publishing orders and subscriptions are not open.

Not an active offer

This page describes a possible future business model. There is no checkout, rate card, order acceptance, subscription, customer dashboard or distribution service available now.

What we may explore later

Future possibility

Company announcements

A possible route for reviewing and clearly labelling relevant business announcements, if the editorial operation and audience support it.

Future possibility

Research report summaries

A possible format for concise, attributed introductions to market research reports, subject to rights, sourcing and reader value.

Long-term product

Customer subscriptions

Only after repeat demand and service value are demonstrated. Any future plan would state its article allowance, dashboard features and terms clearly.

Distribution would need to be specific

The current plan makes no promise of partner websites, RSS, email, social posting, directories, other publications, Google News inclusion or a particular search result. Any channel offered later would need to be active, rights-cleared and named clearly in its terms. Search visibility and third-party pickup cannot be guaranteed.

Editorial independence comes first

Any future paid content would be labelled and kept separate from independent reporting. Payment would not buy newsroom coverage, a favourable conclusion or control over editorial decisions. Read the editorial policy.

Before a service opens

IndustryWire would first establish its editorial workflow, audience evidence, publisher identity, hosting, service terms, privacy practices and distribution capabilities. Subscription credits, expiry, edits, renewals and any customer dashboard would be defined before a customer could buy.

For now, follow the newsroom build

Original reporting is in development. Visit the newsroom status page or read about IndustryWire’s editorial approach.
