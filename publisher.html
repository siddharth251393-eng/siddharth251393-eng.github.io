PUBLISHER

Kavita

Publisher developing The Sector Journal, an independent business and industry newsroom based in India.

Kavita is establishing The Sector Journal’s publication and editorial process. She is the publisher, not the owner of the firm. Ownership is a separate role and is not specified here. This profile does not claim journalism credentials, a reporting team, or a particular story byline.

Authorship

Articles will credit the person who actually wrote or reviewed them. The The Sector Journal Editorial Desk byline may be used for desk-produced work when appropriate. Kavita should be named as an article author only when she has made that contribution.
